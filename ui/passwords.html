Passkeys

Passkeys can’t be copied out of Chrome, Edge, Google Password Manager or other browsers — they’re built so they never leave the place they were made. Sites still let you sign in with them in Wisp: choose Use a phone or tablet and scan the code, or use Windows Hello.

New passkeys you create in Wisp are kept by Windows (or by a password manager extension such as Bitwarden).
