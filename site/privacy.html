Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. This covers Wisp for Windows and Wisp for iPhone and iPad.

In short: Wisp has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Your history, bookmarks, tabs and settings stay on your device. Wisp only contacts other services for features you use, listed below.

What stays on your device

  • Windows: settings, open tabs, history, bookmarks, downloads list and site data are kept in %APPDATA%\Wisp. Private windows use a temporary folder that is deleted when the window closes.
  • Saved passwords (Windows) are kept in %APPDATA%\Wisp, encrypted for your Windows account. Importing from another browser reads its saved passwords on this PC; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • AI keys you add for your own providers are stored in Windows Credential Manager, not in Wisp's files.
  • iPhone and iPad: tabs, bookmarks and settings are kept inside the app. Website data is kept by iOS's WebKit for the app.
  • Wisp does not upload any of this anywhere. Uninstalling Wisp (and deleting %APPDATA%\Wisp on Windows) removes it.

When Wisp talks to other services

Websites you visit receive your requests as in any browser. Beyond that, Wisp itself only connects to:

ServiceWhen, and what is sent
GitHubChecking for updates (Windows). Wisp downloads the release list or update file from github.com. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings → Updates.
Your search engineSearch suggestions (Windows). What you type in the address bar is sent to your chosen engine (DuckDuckGo by default, or Google or Bing) to get suggestions. Turn this off in Settings → General.
EasyList (easylist.to)Downloading and updating the ad and tracker blocking lists (Windows). No personal data is sent. On iPhone the block list is built into the app.
Pollinations.aiOnly when you use the free built-in Wisp AI (Windows). Your messages, and the page's text when you ask about a page, are sent to Pollinations to answer. They are not used by Wisp for anything else.
Chrome Web Store (Google)Only when you use extensions (Windows). Adding or updating an extension downloads it from Google's Chrome Web Store, and the Extensions page downloads store icons for its list of popular extensions. Installed extensions then follow their own privacy policies.
Your AI providerIf you choose Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, messages go to that provider under its own privacy policy. Ollama runs on your own computer.
Open-MeteoOnly if you turn on the weather widget on the New Tab page (Windows). The city name you enter is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the forecast.

What Wisp never does

  • No analytics, crash reporting, usage statistics or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no Wisp server that could receive it.
  • No accounts or sign-in.

The web engine

On Windows, Wisp shows pages with Microsoft Edge WebView2. On iPhone and iPad, it uses Apple's WebKit. Wisp turns off WebView2's SmartScreen and "mini menu" features. Microsoft and Apple update these engines through Windows Update and iOS updates.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The download buttons ask GitHub's public API for the latest version. The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the source repository. Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
